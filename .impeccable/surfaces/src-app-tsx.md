## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is a boxed-software reference manual open at a tabbed division; the CV is the top-ply leaf lying on the board. Refuses the resume-builder default: form column, rounded cards, shrunken paper thumbnail on grey.

OWN-WORLD: Bone section board with print grain. Eight full-strength divider hues on a stepped fore-edge tab rail, tab height proportional to section extent. Chrome yellow reserved by law for commit; vermilion held out for errata. Archivo grotesque caps heads hanging in the fifth column of a four-column grid, Source Serif body, JetBrains Mono machine voice. Hairline rules, punched hinges, one hard shadow per leaf, no easing anywhere: 90ms two-frame steps.

STORY: Open a division by tab or digit key, edit on the hinged leaf, watch the sheet repaginate live, commit one yellow EXPORT.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Bound edge left with punch holes and rotated mono spine naming the profile. The A4 sheet dominates centre at true scale on the board, carrying page-break rules and margin page numbers. Editor leaf right, hinged and shadowed. Tab rail at the far right fore edge, active tab extended and owning the leaf's rubric. Header rail: wordmark, machine-voice readouts PROFILE / FORMAT / PAGES, one chrome-yellow EXPORT PDF.

FORM: Acetate tab board (`rw-manual-acetate-tab-board`), chosen by the user over the assigned roll; assigned index 6 of the grounded list; seed key 95ef39e0.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
